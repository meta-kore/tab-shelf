# Tab Shelf Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Tab Shelf is a browser extension that saves lists of your open tabs so you can reopen them later.

## What data Tab Shelf handles

When you choose to save a window or tab, Tab Shelf reads that tab's **web address (URL)** and **page title**. It stores them, along with the name and date you save them under, so it can show them on your shelf.

## Where the data is stored

All data is stored **only on your device**, in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). Tab Shelf:

- does **not** send your data to any server, including the developer's;
- does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services;
- does **not** sell or share your data with anyone;
- does **not** sync your data between devices.

Website icons are displayed using the browser's built-in favicon service, which reads them from your browser's own cache.

## Export and import

If you use **Export backup**, Tab Shelf creates a file on your computer containing your saved tabs. What happens to that file is up to you. **Import** reads a file you choose; its contents stay on your device.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual tabs or windows from the shelf page, or remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes all data it stored.
